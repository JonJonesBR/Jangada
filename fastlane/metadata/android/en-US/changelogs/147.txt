OpenCode chat: economy preset, switched on in Settings or per job (⋮ menu, settings and context), with a cheap Go plan model for background agents and earlier compaction, in an app-owned file; your opencode.json is not changed. Answer length: general and per-job setting, from normal to very short. Chat: long-press the text to select a snippet, and use the Copy button next to the time for the whole answer. Agents can call each other: the jangada-call command runs another agent for one turn with a role (reviewer, planner, implementer or orchestrator) and the cheap model for support roles; off by default, writing roles only with Approve all on, and the answer shows as a card in the chat.
